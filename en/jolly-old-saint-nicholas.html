About the piece

The words come from a poem by Emily Huntington Miller, published in December 1865 as “Lilly's Secret” in the children's magazine The Little Corporal: a child asks Saint Nicholas to whisper what he will bring for Christmas.

Who wrote the tune is not certain: it is credited either to James R. Murray or to Benjamin Hanby. Our arrangement follows the best-known version, in B♭ major.

The arrangement was written by us for Piano Learning; the app also has a ★★ version with chords.

How to practise it

  1. Read the notes above and find the hand position: most of the time the fingers stay on five neighbouring keys.
  2. Each phrase starts with four repeated notes (D D D D, G G G G): play them with the same finger, lightly, counting “one-and two-and”.
  3. Add the left hand only when the right hand is secure, first at 60% as in the video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to read music?

No: the notes are written in letters and the video shows which key to press and for how long.

Can I play it with one hand?

Yes, the right-hand melody alone is already recognisable. In the app, if you practise one hand, the app plays the other.
