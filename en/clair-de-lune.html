About the piece

It is the third of the four pieces of the Suite bergamasque, begun around 1890 and published only in 1905. The title comes from a poem by Paul Verlaine.

It starts softly, almost outside time, with chords in 9/8; the middle section comes alive with continuous left-hand arpeggios under a broad melody.

How to practise it

  1. The opening: chords in both hands, very soft. Count the rests and tied notes carefully: the magic is in the suspended time.
  2. The middle section: learn the left-hand arpeggios alone and slowly first, then add the melody.
  3. The pedal is essential: change it with the harmony, listening for a clean sound.
  4. Split the piece into parts (the app splits it into four) and work on one part at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

Not straight away: it needs control of sound and pedal. The first page is slow, though, and can be tackled early, hands separately.

Is the sheet music free?

Yes: Debussy died in 1918, the music is in the public domain and this Mutopia Project edition is free.
