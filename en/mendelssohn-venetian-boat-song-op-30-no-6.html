About the piece

Felix Mendelssohn wrote 48 Songs without Words (Lieder ohne Worte), piano pieces built like songs: a melody singing over an accompaniment. The second book, Op. 30, came out in 1835.

Very few of the titles are Mendelssohn's own: among them are the three «Venetian Boat Songs» (Op. 19 No. 6, Op. 30 No. 6 and Op. 62 No. 5). In the one from Op. 30, in F-sharp minor, the left hand imitates the oars with the rocking 6/8 rhythm.

How to practise it

  1. The left hand first. It is the movement of the gondola: it must be steady and soft, so practise it alone until it runs by itself.
  2. The melody on top must sing: give it more weight than the rest, even when the right hand plays several notes together.
  3. Clean pedalling: change it with the harmony so the bass notes do not blur.
  4. Hands together slowly, at 60% as in the video, then speed up a little at a time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a barcarolle?

It is the song of the Venetian gondoliers; in music it means a piece with a rocking rhythm (6/8 or 12/8) that recalls the motion of the boat.

Which of the three boat songs should I learn?

They are similar in difficulty, all intermediate: start with the one you like best.
