About the piece

It belongs to the 24 Preludes Op. 28 (1839). It is so essential that other composers built whole works on it: Rachmaninoff used it as the theme of his Variations on a Theme of Chopin Op. 22.

Every bar has four full chords in both hands; after the loud first phrase, the others repeat soft and very soft.

How to practise it

  1. Learn the chords one at a time, looking at the finger position before you play.
  2. Use the weight of your arm, don't hit: full but not harsh sound.
  3. The change from loud to soft is the heart of the piece: exaggerate it.
  4. Change the pedal on every chord, just after playing it.

The notes are slow, but four-note chords in each hand need some stretch and confidence.
