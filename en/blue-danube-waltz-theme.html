About the piece

Johann Strauss II wrote it in 1867; originally a choral piece, it quickly became Vienna's most famous waltz. Every year it closes the Vienna Philharmonic's New Year's Concert.

This version presents the main theme: melody in the right hand and the classic waltz accompaniment in the left.

How to practise it

  1. The left hand does the waltz step: bass on beat one, lighter chords on two and three.
  2. The melody climbs up the D major chord: learn the first four bars well.
  3. Think “in one”: a single pulse per bar, for lift.
  4. It is short: great as a first piece to play from memory.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the complete version?

No, it is the main theme; the full orchestral waltz has five waltzes and a coda.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes, the theme is approachable after a few months of study.
