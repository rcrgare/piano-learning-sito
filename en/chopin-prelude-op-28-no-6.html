About the piece

Chopin completed the 24 Preludes Op. 28 in 1839, largely during the winter in Majorca, and published them the same year: one for each major and minor key.

No. 6 is marked Lento assai. Together with No. 4 it was played on the organ at Chopin's funeral, at the Madeleine church in Paris, in 1849.

How to practise it

  1. The left hand first, on its own. It has the melody: play it legato and singing, as if it were breathing.
  2. The right hand accompanies with repeated chords: keep them soft and always quieter than the left hand.
  3. Hands together slowly, listening that the melody always stays in the foreground.
  4. Use the pedal carefully and change it often, so the left-hand notes do not blur.

Frequently asked questions

Is it difficult?

There are few notes and the tempo is slow: it is one of the best preludes after a couple of years of lessons. The real challenge is the sound, not the technique.

Why is the melody in the left hand?

Chopin chose it to get a dark colour, like a cello. It is also a great exercise for a hand that usually accompanies.
