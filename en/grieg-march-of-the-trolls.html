About the piece

Edvard Grieg published the March of the Trolls (Norwegian Trolltog) in 1891, in the fifth book of his Lyric Pieces, Op. 54. The Lyric Pieces are 66 short piano works that appeared in ten books between 1867 and 1901.

The piece alternates a march in D minor, with strong accents and fast figures, with a slow, singing middle section in D major. Grieg also included it, orchestrated, in his Lyric Suite. In English it is also known as March of the Dwarfs.

How to practise it

  1. Slowly at first. Practise the fast right-hand figures at 50-60% speed: accuracy first, then speed.
  2. The accents give the piece its character: make them clear, but without stiffening the wrist.
  3. The middle section asks for the opposite: soft tone, legato and clean pedalling. Practise it on its own.
  4. In the left-hand leaps, look at the keyboard early and move the hand before you play the note.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

No: it is an upper-intermediate piece. Start with easier Lyric Pieces first, such as the Arietta Op. 12 No. 1.

What are trolls?

Creatures of Scandinavian folklore that live in mountains and forests. Grieg portrays them also in «In the Hall of the Mountain King», from Peer Gynt.
