About the piece

Charles-Louis Hanon published The Virtuoso Pianist in 1873: sixty exercises to make every finger even and agile. The first one is the most famous.

The same eight-note figure climbs step by step up the keyboard, with the hands an octave apart, and then comes down.

How to practise it

  1. Slowly and with a metronome: the goal is evenness between the fingers, not speed.
  2. Relaxed wrist and arm: if you feel tension, stop.
  3. A few minutes a day are enough; better as a warm-up than for half an hour.
  4. Once you know it, try it soft, loud, staccato and legato.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hanon really help?

Used in moderation and without tension, it helps finger independence; many teachers recommend it as a warm-up.

How often should I play it?

A few minutes a day, always relaxed.
