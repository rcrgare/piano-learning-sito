About the piece

The Ballade is No. 15 of the 25 Easy and Progressive Studies Op. 100, published in 1851 and still used in music schools all over the world, like Arabesque.

The opening theme lies in the low register, under detached right-hand chords; the middle section moves to C major with a more singing melody, before the theme returns.

How to practise it

  1. The staccato. The repeated chords must be short and light: let the wrist bounce, without pushing from the arm.
  2. The low theme must be clearly heard: it is the main voice, even though it is in the bass.
  3. The middle section is more legato: change your touch, as if a new character entered the story.
  4. Hands together at 60%, then speed up only when the staccato stays clean.

Frequently asked questions

What level do I need?

It is an easy-intermediate study: if you have played Arabesque or other pieces of Op. 100, you are ready.

Why is it called “Ballade”?

As in narrative ballads, the music seems to tell an adventurous, slightly dark story, with a calmer episode in the middle.
