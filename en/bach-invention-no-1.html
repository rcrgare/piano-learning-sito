About the piece

Bach wrote the 15 Two-Part Inventions to teach “a singing style” in two voices; the fair copy dates from 1723. He had also prepared them for his son Wilhelm Friedemann.

The eight-note theme appears in the right hand, then in the left, inverted, transposed: the whole piece grows from it.

How to practise it

  1. Learn the theme and spot it in the score every time it returns.
  2. Hands separately, then together slowly: both voices are equally important.
  3. Light staccato or legato? Both are fine, but choose one and stay consistent.
  4. Low speed with the app, then speed up.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Inventions for beginners?

They are study pieces: No. 1 is among the first people tackle, after the minuets of the Anna Magdalena Notebook.

What does “two-part” mean?

Two independent melodic lines, one per hand.
