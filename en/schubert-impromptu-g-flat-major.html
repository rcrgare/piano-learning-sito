About the piece

Schubert wrote the four Impromptus D 899 in 1827, a year before he died. The first two came out at once; the third and fourth were published only in 1857.

In that edition the publisher Haslinger transposed the third Impromptu to G major, which is easier to read. Today it is played in the original key, G♭ major, with six flats.

How to practise it

  1. The melody is at the top of the right hand: play it louder than the accompanying notes of the same hand.
  2. The accompaniment should flow evenly and softly, like a river under the voice.
  3. Change the pedal at every change of harmony, no more, so the sound does not blur.

Frequently asked questions

Why six flats?

It is Schubert's original key, G♭ major. In the app and in the video every note is shown one by one anyway.

What is an impromptu?

A piano piece of free, almost improvised character, short or medium in length.
