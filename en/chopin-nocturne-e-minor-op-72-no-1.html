About the piece

Chopin composed it around 1827, still in Warsaw, but did not publish it: it came out in 1855, after his death, as Op. 72.

The left hand spreads wide triplet arpeggios, the right sings a simple melody that grows richer towards the end.

How to practise it

  1. The left-hand arpeggios are the foundation: practise them alone until they flow effortlessly.
  2. Play the melody singing, over a lighter left hand.
  3. Watch out for the “3 against 2” spots between the hands: slowly at first.
  4. Pedal at every change of bass.

Frequently asked questions

Is it easier than Op. 9 No. 2?

Yes, it is generally considered more approachable: fewer ornaments and a simple structure.

Why is it “posthumous”?

Because it was published after Chopin's death in 1849.
