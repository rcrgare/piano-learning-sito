About the piece

It is the seventh of the thirteen pieces of Kinderszenen (Scenes from Childhood) Op. 15, written in 1838: childhood memories seen by an adult, not music for children.

The melody moves between voices and the hands share the chords: a little exercise in singing several voices at once.

How to practise it

  1. Find the melody in the video: sometimes it is on top, sometimes in an inner voice.
  2. Hold the long notes while the others move: fingers “glued” to the keys.
  3. Light pedal, changed with every harmony.
  4. The opening phrase returns several times: polish every detail of it.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Scenes from Childhood for children?

No: Schumann described them as an adult's memories for adults. Some pieces are approachable at intermediate level, though.

What does Träumerei mean?

“Reverie”, “daydreaming”.
