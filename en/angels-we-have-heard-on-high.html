About the piece

It is a French Christmas carol, “Les Anges dans nos campagnes”: it tells of the angels singing to the shepherds, as in the Gospel of Luke. The English words are a paraphrase by Bishop James Chadwick.

Its best-known moment is the “Gloria in excelsis Deo” refrain: on the “o” of “Gloria” the melody rises and falls through 16 notes in a row, a melisma you play with the right hand in our arrangement.

The arrangement was written by us for Piano Learning; the app also has a ★★ version with chords.

How to practise it

  1. Read the notes above and find the hand position: most of the time the fingers stay on five neighbouring keys.
  2. In the “Gloria” the eighth notes come down in groups of four (C B♭ A, B♭ A G…): play each group smoothly, without stopping between groups.
  3. Add the left hand only when the right hand is secure, first at 60% as in the video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to read music?

No: the notes are written in letters and the video shows which key to press and for how long.

Can I play it with one hand?

Yes, the right-hand melody alone is already recognisable. In the app, if you practise one hand, the app plays the other.
