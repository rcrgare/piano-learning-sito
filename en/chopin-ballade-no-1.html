About the piece

Chopin began the Ballade in Vienna in 1831 and finished it in Paris; it was published in 1836 with a dedication to Baron Nathaniel von Stockhausen. Robert Schumann reported that Chopin counted it among his favourite works.

It was the first of four ballades: long, free pieces without words that tell a story through music alone. After the slow introduction comes the main theme, at an easy-going tempo; the finale, Presto con fuoco, is one of the most demanding passages in the repertoire.

How to practise it

  1. Split the piece into sections: introduction, first theme, second theme, development and coda. Practise one section at a time.
  2. The first theme should sing: the right hand must be heard above the light chords of the left.
  3. Fast passages are practised very slowly and hands separately, then in groups of a few notes.
  4. Do not rush to the coda: it is the hardest part and is best left for last.

Frequently asked questions

How long and how hard is it?

It lasts about ten minutes and is an advanced piece. With the app, though, you can practise it one passage at a time and slowed down, starting with the first theme.

Where have I heard it?

It is one of Chopin's most performed works; it is also heard in Roman Polański's film The Pianist (2002).
