About the piece

Von fremden Ländern und Menschen is the first of the thirteen pieces of Kinderszenen (Scenes from Childhood) Op. 15, written by Schumann in 1838. The composer himself described them as an adult's memories meant for adults, rather than music for children.

The melody is at the top of the right hand; below it runs an accompaniment in triplets shared between the hands, over a simple bass. The famous Träumerei belongs to the same collection.

How to practise it

  1. Find the melody. First play only the top notes of the right hand, singing along: that is the voice that must stand out.
  2. The triplets of the accompaniment pass from one hand to the other: practise them slowly and keep them softer than the melody.
  3. Hands together at 60% as in the video, bar by bar.
  4. A little pedal helps to connect, but change it with every harmony so the triplets do not blur.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, the level is approachable after a couple of years of lessons. But it is music meant for adults too: people of every age love it.

How long is it?

Under a minute with the repeat: a great piece to learn in full in a few weeks.
