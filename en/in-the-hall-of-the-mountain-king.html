About the piece

Grieg wrote it in 1875 for Peer Gynt, Henrik Ibsen's play: it accompanies the scene where the hero enters the hall of the trolls. He later included it in the first orchestral suite, Op. 46.

The whole piece is built on a single repeated melody that starts softly in the bass and grows to a thunderous ending.

How to practise it

  1. Learn the theme (the first two bars) until you play it without thinking: you will find it everywhere.
  2. At the start everything is staccato and soft: short, light notes.
  3. Build the crescendo and accelerando little by little: don't get loud too early.
  4. Practise the ending at reduced speed: full chords in both hands.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

The theme is very simple; the complete version with the fast ending is intermediate.

Who is the Mountain King?

The king of the trolls from Norwegian folklore, in Ibsen's play.
