About the piece

It is the third movement of the Sonata in A major K 331, written around 1783. Mozart marked it Alla turca: it imitates the Ottoman Janissary bands, very fashionable in Vienna, with their drums and cymbals.

In the A major section the left hand plays very fast broken chords that sound just like a drum roll.

How to practise it

  1. The A minor theme (the opening bars) hands separately: the right-hand notes are fast but repetitive.
  2. The left-hand chords in the major section need a loose wrist, never stiff.
  3. Practise at 50% until the triplets and little scales are perfectly even.
  4. The piece is made of repeating sections: once you learn one, you already have several.
