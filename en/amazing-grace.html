About the piece

The words were written by former slave trader John Newton and published in 1779; the tune now used, called “New Britain”, appeared in the United States in 1835.

It is one of the best-known hymns in the world, slow and solemn.

The arrangement was written by us for Piano Learning; the app also has a ★★ version with chords.

How to practise it

  1. Read the notes above and find the hand position: most of the time the fingers stay on five neighbouring keys.
  2. Hold the long notes for their full value: it is a hymn, it must breathe.
  3. Add the left hand only when the right hand is secure, first at 60% as in the video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to read music?

No: the notes are written in letters and the video shows which key to press and for how long.

Can I play it with one hand?

Yes, the right-hand melody alone is already recognisable. In the app, if you practise one hand, the app plays the other.
