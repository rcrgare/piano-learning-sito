About the piece

The tune comes from Good Morning to All, a school song by sisters Mildred and Patty Hill published in 1893. For decades it was at the centre of a copyright dispute that ended in 2016: today it is in the public domain.

It is the most sung song in the world: knowing how to play it comes in handy at every party.

The arrangement was written by us for Piano Learning; the app also has a ★★ version with chords.

The notes of the melody (right hand)

Notes with a coloured border are longer. The video also shows the left hand.

How to practise it

  1. Read the notes above and find the hand position: most of the time the fingers stay on five neighbouring keys.
  2. The biggest leap is at the start of the third phrase: prepare it by moving your hand early.
  3. Add the left hand only when the right hand is secure, first at 60% as in the video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to read music?

No: the notes are written in letters and the video shows which key to press and for how long.

Can I play it with one hand?

Yes, the right-hand melody alone is already recognisable. In the app, if you practise one hand, the app plays the other.
