About the piece

It is the sixth piece of the eighth book of Lyric Pieces, Op. 65, published in 1896. Troldhaugen is the villa of Edvard Grieg and his wife Nina near Bergen, in Norway, now a museum.

The piece is linked to the couple's silver wedding anniversary (they married in 1867): the march recalls the friends arriving to celebrate, the middle section is more intimate and songlike.

How to practise it

  1. The march needs a firm rhythm: accent the first beat and keep the dotted notes precise.
  2. The left-hand chords should be prepared in advance: look at the position before they come.
  3. In the middle section change character: softer and legato, letting the melody sing.
