About the piece

Chopin published it in 1847 and dedicated it to Countess Delfina Potocka. “Minute” means “small”, not “sixty seconds”: played well it lasts almost twice that. It is said to have been inspired by a little dog chasing its own tail.

The right hand races through very fast circular figures, while the left keeps the classic waltz step: bass and two chords.

How to practise it

  1. Speed at 40–50% at first: the right-hand figures must be even and light.
  2. Left hand alone, bass–chord–chord, with a loose wrist.
  3. The middle section is slower and singing: a good place to start.
  4. Speed up only when a passage is clean three times in a row.

Frequently asked questions

Does it really last a minute?

No: “minute” here means “small”. It usually takes between a minute and a half and two minutes.

Is it suitable for beginners?

No, it is advanced because of the fast right hand. The middle section can be learned earlier, though.
