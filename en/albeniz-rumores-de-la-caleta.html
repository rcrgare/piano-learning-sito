About the piece

It is the sixth of the “Recuerdos de viaje” (Travel Memories) Op. 71, a set of pieces the Spanish composer Isaac Albéniz published around 1887. La Caleta is a seaside district of Málaga.

The subtitle “Malagueña” refers to an Andalusian folk song: you can hear the quick repeated chords of the flamenco guitar and, in the middle section, a free and melancholy melody.

How to practise it

  1. The repeated chords imitate the guitar: keep them short and light, with a loose wrist.
  2. The middle section is a song: here you can slow down a little and let the phrases breathe.
  3. Practise the fast passages hands separately first, then put them together slowly.

Frequently asked questions

What is a malagueña?

An Andalusian folk song from the Málaga area, related to the fandango and accompanied by the guitar.

How hard is it?

It is intermediate: the fast passages need precision, but the structure is clear and it is learnt section by section.
