About the piece

Mozart entered it in his catalogue in 1788 as “a little sonata for beginners”. The nicknames “facile” or “semplice” came later; in fact it needs agile, even fingers.

The left hand uses the “Alberti bass” (a chord broken low–high–middle–high), the right alternates a singing melody with long scales.

How to practise it

  1. The Alberti bass alone: light, with the first note just slightly stronger.
  2. The right-hand scales at reduced speed, checking the fingering (thumb crossings).
  3. Hands together on the opening theme, then one section at a time.
  4. Clear sound and little pedal: this is transparent Viennese classical music.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really easy?

It is one of Mozart's most approachable sonatas, but the fast scales need a couple of years of study.

What does K 545 mean?

It is the number in the Köchel catalogue, the list of Mozart's works.
