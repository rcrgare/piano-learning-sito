About the piece

It is the second of the Deux Arabesques published in 1891. Marked Allegretto scherzando, it has a light, playful character.

The right hand opens with quick falling triplets, the left answers with staccato chords; the middle section is more singing.

How to practise it

  1. Practise the opening triplets slowly: light, with fingers close to the keys.
  2. The left-hand staccato must be short and precise.
  3. In the middle section play more legato and use a little pedal.
  4. Speed up only when the triplets are even.

Is it harder than No. 1?

About the same level, but it needs more agility and precision in the staccato.
