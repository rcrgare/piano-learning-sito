About the piece

Tchaikovsky composed the Album for the Young Op. 39 in 1878: 24 short pieces for young pianists, dedicated to his nephew Vladimir Davydov. His stated model was Schumann's Album for the Young.

The Old French Song is No. 16 of the set: a simple melody in G minor over a calm accompaniment, with a clear form that brings the theme back at the end.

How to practise it

  1. Sing the melody. The right hand must connect each note to the next, like a voice: try humming it while you play.
  2. A soft left hand. Play the accompaniment more quietly than the melody, without detaching the notes.
  3. Let the phrases breathe: at the end of each phrase lighten the sound, like someone taking a breath.
  4. It is a great piece for learning the pedal: change it with the harmony, right after playing the bass note.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes, for those with a few months of practice: there are few notes, the real challenge is playing it in a singing way.

How long is it?

About one minute: perfect for a recital or for learning a complete piece from start to finish.
