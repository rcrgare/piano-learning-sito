About the piece

Tchaikovsky composed the Album for the Young Op. 39 in 1878: 24 short pieces for young pianists, dedicated to his nephew Vladimir Davydov and modelled on Schumann's Album for the Young.

The March of the Wooden Soldiers is No. 5 of the set: short, detached chords, a dotted marching rhythm and a melody that repeats with small changes.

How to practise it

  1. The dotted rhythm is the heart of the piece: count it out loud («long, short») before you play it.
  2. Light staccato: the fingers bounce off the keys, the wrist stays relaxed. No pedal.
  3. Steady tempo: it is a march, the speed must not change. A metronome helps.
  4. Hands together starting at 60%, as in the video.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes, for those with a few months of practice: it is short, and the main challenge is keeping a steady rhythm.

How long is it?

About forty seconds: great for a recital.
