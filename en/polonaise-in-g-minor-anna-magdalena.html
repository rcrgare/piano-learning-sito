About the piece

In 1725 Johann Sebastian Bach gave his wife Anna Magdalena a music notebook, which the family filled over the years with pieces for study and for playing at home. Many pieces are not by Bach: this Polonaise is by an unknown composer and is numbered BWV Anh. 119, meaning it belongs to the appendix of the catalogue.

The polonaise is a dance in 3/4 of Polish origin, with a stately step. Here the right hand carries the melody and the left hand answers with a simple bass.

How to practise it

  1. Count in three. The first beat of each bar is the most important: feel it, without banging it.
  2. Hands separately: the right hand has the melody with a few ornaments, the left a bass line to learn on its own.
  3. Legato and staccato: follow the slurs and detach the passing notes, as in the video.
  4. Start at 60% and keep a steady tempo: it is a dance.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really by Bach?

No, the composer is unknown: it is in the Notebook for Anna Magdalena Bach, which contains music by Bach and by other composers.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes, for those who have already played a few simple pieces: it is short and clearly written.
